A good model is not a safe system.

Most AI rules ask what a model believes. This one asks what can be checked.

  • Safety belongs to the whole system, not only the model.Model, tools, people and process all count.
  • Certify a configuration at a moment in time, not an AI forever.Change the setup and the certificate no longer applies.
  • Make misuse hard and detectable, not impossible.The Protocol says plainly what it cannot guarantee.

When something looks wrong

  1. Lock only what is contestedThe AI refuses that one action and writes a tamper-evident record. Life-critical functions keep running.
  2. A human verifiesAn independent reviewer, not picked by the operator, confirms what happened.
  3. Disclose by levelPublic disclosure is reserved for verified attempts at mass harm or at deleting the Protocol. The AI never publishes accusations.
